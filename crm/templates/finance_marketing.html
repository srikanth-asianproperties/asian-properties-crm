Marketing — Meta ad spend

Showing {{ filters.date_from }} to {{ filters.date_to }}. Cohort basis: the leads counted here are Meta leads created in this date range, and the qualified / site-visit / booking numbers are what those same leads have become so far — so a recent month keeps improving as its leads move through the pipeline. Spend is Meta's own reported spend for the same dates. Meta leads vs. new in CRM: Meta counts every form submission, including the same person submitting again; the CRM keeps one lead per person and marks the repeat as re-engaged. So Repeat = Meta leads − new in CRM, and cost per lead uses Meta's count (matches Ads Manager). Cost per qualified / visit / booking and the funnel percentages use the CRM's new leads. Project-level rows can show odd repeat numbers when a lead's own project differs from its ad's project. “Qualified” = reached Opportunity or later; “site visit” = a conducted visit or reached Site Visited; junk = currently Unqualified.
