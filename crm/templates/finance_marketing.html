Marketing — Meta ad spend

Showing {{ filters.date_from }} to {{ filters.date_to }}. Cohort basis: the leads counted here are Meta leads created in this date range, and the qualified / site-visit / booking numbers are what those same leads have become so far — so a recent month keeps improving as its leads move through the pipeline. Spend is Meta's own reported spend for the same dates. Meta leads vs. new in CRM: Meta counts every form submission, including the same person submitting again; the CRM keeps one lead per person and marks the repeat as re-engaged. So Repeat = Meta leads − new in CRM, shown as a count and as a % of Meta leads (a high % is normal and is not a warning). Only a negative Repeat (Meta counted fewer leads than the CRM created) is flagged “possible missed leads — check Job A”; a dash means Meta's count isn't available. Cost per lead uses Meta's count (matches Ads Manager). Cost per qualified / visit / booking and the funnel percentages use the CRM's new leads. “Qualified” = reached Opportunity or later; “site visit” = a conducted visit or reached Site Visited. Projects: leads are counted under the project of the ad campaign that brought them in, even if the CRM later moved them to another project. Rejected at first call counts leads ever marked Unqualified; the CRM cannot tell whether it was literally the first call. A lead can be both rejected (for one project) and qualified (for another). Passed to other project (campaign table) = leads the CRM moved to another project after they were rejected.
